Scheme: Diesel Subsidy for Motorized Fishing Vessels
Category: Fuel cost relief for registered fishers

Overview:
Several coastal states offer a partial subsidy (or a diesel price
differential / VAT relief) on the diesel used by registered motorized
fishing boats, to reduce the operating cost of marine fishing trips.
Exact scheme names, subsidy rates, and yearly fuel quotas differ by
state (e.g. rates and quota per boat class vary between Maharashtra,
Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and other coastal states).

Who can apply:
- Owners/operators of registered motorized fishing vessels
- The vessel must typically hold a valid fishing license/registration
  with the state Fisheries Department
- Some states require the applicant to be an active, resident fisher
  (not just a boat owner) with a valid fisher ID card

What it covers:
- A subsidy amount per litre of diesel, up to an annual quota per
  vessel/horsepower class
- Some states route this as a direct cash reimbursement instead of a
  point-of-sale discount

How to apply (general process):
1. Register the vessel and obtain a fishing license from the state
   Fisheries Department if not already done.
2. Apply for a diesel subsidy card / quota allocation, usually at the
   local Fisheries office or through the state's fisheries web portal.
3. Fuel purchases are recorded (via subsidy card, coupon, or receipts)
   and reimbursement or point-of-sale discount is applied up to the
   annual quota.

Note: because quotas, per-litre rates, and the application portal are
state-specific and revised periodically, always confirm current figures
with the local Fisheries Department or state fisheries welfare portal
before relying on this summary.
