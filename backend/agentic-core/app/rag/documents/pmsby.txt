Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
Category: Accidental death and disability insurance

Overview:
PMSBY is a low-premium accident insurance scheme open to all Indian
residents, including fishers, that provides a payout to the nominee in
case of accidental death, and a payout to the insured in case of
permanent or partial disability caused by an accident (including
accidents at sea).

Who can apply:
- Any Indian resident aged 18 to 70 with a bank or post-office account
- Enrollment is usually done through the bank account via auto-debit of
  the annual premium

What it covers:
- A lump-sum payout to nominees in the event of accidental death
- A payout for total permanent disability (e.g. loss of both eyes or limbs)
- A reduced payout for partial permanent disability

How to apply (general process):
1. Visit your bank branch (or use net banking / the bank's app) and opt
   in to PMSBY -- most banks show this as a one-time consent form.
2. The small annual premium is auto-debited from the linked account each
   year during the renewal window; keep sufficient balance to avoid the
   policy lapsing.
3. In the event of a claim, the nominee should approach the bank branch
   with the death certificate / medical disability certificate and
   required KYC documents.

Note: this is a general summary. Premium amount, payout amount, and
renewal dates are set by the government and can change year to year --
confirm the current figures with your bank or a Common Service Centre.
