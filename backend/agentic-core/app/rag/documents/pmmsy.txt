Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)
Category: Fisheries development and welfare subsidy

Overview:
PMMSY is a central government scheme aimed at developing India's fisheries
sector, raising fish production and productivity, modernizing the fish
value chain, and improving the income of fishers and fish farmers.

Who can apply:
- Individual fishers and fish farmers (marine and inland)
- Fisher cooperative societies and self-help groups
- Fish Farmer Producer Organisations (FFPOs)
- Entrepreneurs setting up fisheries-related infrastructure

What it covers:
- Support for new fishing vessels, safety equipment, and gear upgrades
- Assistance for cold-chain, ice plants, and post-harvest infrastructure
- Support for aquaculture pond construction and modernization
- Insurance and welfare support components for fishers

How to apply (general process):
1. Contact the local Fisheries Department office or State Fisheries
   Corporation for the current application window.
2. Submit identity proof, proof of fishing occupation (e.g. boat
   registration or fisher ID card), and bank account details.
3. Applications are typically reviewed at the state level, with subsidy
   disbursed directly to the beneficiary's bank account (DBT) after
   approval.

Note: exact subsidy percentages, eligibility income limits, and
application windows vary by state and are updated periodically. Always
confirm current details with the local Fisheries Department before
relying on this summary for a real application.
